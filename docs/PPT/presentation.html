Slide 01 — Problem Statement

What's Broken in Car Rental Today?

01
Fragmented & Outdated Digital Experiences
Existing rental platforms are cluttered, slow, and offer no sense of brand identity. Users abandon bookings due to friction-heavy multi-step flows.
02
No Curation — Overwhelming Vehicle Choice
Platforms dump hundreds of vehicles on users with minimal filtering. High-value customers want a curated, editorial selection — not a spreadsheet.
03
Poor Account & Booking Management
Most services lack proper user profiles, rental history, saved vehicles, or transparent pricing breakdowns — leading to mistrust and poor retention.
04
No Personalization for Premium Customers
The premium segment (sports cars, luxury EVs, exotic SUVs) has no dedicated digital home. Bookings happen over phone calls or via outdated portals.
Slide 02 — Proposed Solution

Introducing outDrive

A full-stack premium vehicle rental platform that delivers a curated fleet, transparent pricing, seamless booking, and complete account management — all from a single cohesive web experience.

🎯
Curated Fleet
Hand-selected sports cars, luxury EVs, and premium SUVs — each with detailed specs and gallery.
⚡
Instant Booking
Multi-step booking flow with real-time pricing calculation, extras selection, and confirmation.
🔐
Secure Accounts
JWT-authenticated profiles with rental history, saved vehicles, and booking management.

Slide 05 — Final Product Features

What outDrive Delivers

🚗
Curated Fleet Browser
Filter by type, seats, price, fuel. Sort by price. Real-time search with MongoDB queries.
📋
Vehicle Detail Pages
Multi-image gallery, full specs, features list, and direct booking CTA per vehicle.
📅
Multi-Step Booking Flow
Date picker, location, extras (insurance, GPS, child seat), live price breakdown.
✅
Booking Confirmation
Unique reference code (OD-XXXX-XXXX), full receipt with tax and service fee.
🔑
Secure Auth System
Sign up / sign in with JWT tokens, bcrypt hashing, 7-day session persistence.
👤
User Profile & Edit
View and update name, email, phone, nationality. Protected by auth middleware.
📂
Rental History
View all past, upcoming, and completed rentals. Cancel upcoming bookings inline.
❤️
Saved Vehicles
Bookmark / unbookmark vehicles. Persisted to MongoDB and returned on login.
